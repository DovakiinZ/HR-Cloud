# Product Vision

> Up: [[Research Index]] · Domain: [[DOMAIN_MAP]]

## Two identities, one codebase
- **"SanadHR" HR Operating System** (README, [[CLAUDE]]) — a full, configuration-first HR platform; every process configurable, no hardcoded logic.
- **"سند / Sanad" reconciliation engine** (marketing landing `src/app/page.tsx`) — pitched as a **payroll-reconciliation engine** across Saudi government platforms (Qiwa/قوى, Mudad/مُدد, GOSI/التأمينات), validating the **WPS/SIF wage-protection file** before bank transfer, with WhatsApp/Apple Messages ESS and an EOS labor-law simulator (Articles 84 & 85).

Both describe the same system from different angles. The HR-OS is the platform; the reconciliation engine is the wedge/positioning.

## The architectural thesis
The product's bet is that **HR processes reduce to reusable engines** — money to the [[Financial Calculation Engine]], configuration to the [[Master Data Engine]], approvals to the [[Workflow Engine]], reproducibility to [[Snapshot and Versioning]]. Payroll is the proof: it's an *app on the engine*, not bespoke code.
