# GulfHR
> A long-standing GCC HR & payroll suite for mid-market — established regional compliance, more traditional UX.
> Up: [[Competitor Index]] · System: [[COMPETITORS]]

## Overview
- **Company:** gulfHR — an established GCC HR/payroll software vendor with a long track record across the Gulf (KSA, UAE, and wider GCC). Positioned as a proven, region-specific incumbent for mid-market employers. 🔄 verify current ownership/HQ and exact founding year.
- **Product:** Integrated HR & payroll suite — core HR, GCC/KSA payroll (GOSI/WPS), time & attendance, leave, ESS/MSS, org management, letters/documents, and standard reporting. 🔄 verify full current module list.
- **Target Market:** GCC mid-market and larger SMBs across multiple GCC countries and multi-country payroll needs. Sells on regional compliance + longevity/stability.
- **Pricing:** Enterprise/mid-market license or SaaS + implementation; not public. 🔄 verify model (on-prem vs cloud tenancy).
- **Strengths:** Deep, mature GCC payroll & statutory compliance (multi-country GCC), long operating history and reference base, region-specific know-how (GOSI/WPS, GCC labor rules), Arabic support.
- **Weaknesses:** More traditional/dated UX vs modern entrants (PalmHR/ZenHR/HiBob); likely heavier configuration; automation/AI/analytics and mobile modernity trail newer products; payroll transparency is conventional (not immutable-ledger). 🔄 verify current UX refresh state.
- **Positioning:** "The proven GCC HR & payroll platform" — stability, regional compliance depth, and multi-country GCC coverage over shiny UX.
