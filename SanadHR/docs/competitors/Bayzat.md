# Bayzat

> The UAE all-in-one — HR + payroll fused with health insurance and employee benefits, its differentiating edge.
> Up: [[Competitor Index]] · System: [[COMPETITORS]]

## Overview
- **Company:** Bayzat — Dubai-headquartered, well-funded UAE HR-tech company that expanded from a health-insurance comparison/benefits origin into all-in-one HR + payroll. Strong UAE brand, expanding into KSA. 🔄 verify funding/scale specifics.
- **Product:** All-in-one work-life platform: HR core, payroll (UAE WPS-first), attendance/leave, and — uniquely — integrated **health insurance + benefits** management, plus a strong mobile-first employee app.
- **Target Market:** UAE (and GCC) SMB→mid; companies that want HR + payroll + insurance/benefits in one place. Expanding KSA footprint. 🔄 verify KSA depth.
- **Pricing:** Per-employee-per-month SaaS tiers; benefits/insurance monetized alongside. 🔄 verify exact tiers/PEPM.
- **Strengths:** Unique insurance/benefits integration (real differentiator); polished mobile-first employee app; strong UAE WPS/payroll; clean modern UX; good SMB onboarding.
- **Weaknesses:** UAE-centric — KSA compliance (GOSI/Mudad/Qiwa, Saudi End-of-Service) is less deep than KSA-native rivals; payroll not transparently reproducible; benefits focus can overshadow HR-core depth; configurability/reporting mid-tier; multi-country Saudi labor-law nuance is a gap vs [[Jisr]]/[[Menaitech]].
- **Positioning:** "One app for pay, benefits, and health insurance." Benefits/insurance bundling as the moat.

## UX Notes
- **Navigation:** Modern, clean, mobile-first design language.
- **Search:** Adequate.
- **Dashboard:** Attractive; surfaces benefits/insurance alongside HR.
- **Configuration:** Friendly; bounded.
- **Automation:** Basic rule-based approvals/reminders.
- **Performance:** Snappy modern app.
- **Accessibility:** Standard.
- **Dark Mode:** 🔄 verify.
- **Arabic Support:** Present; UAE market is bilingual but English-leaning — Arabic/RTL polish is likely weaker than KSA-native rivals, an opening for superior [[Arabic RTL]].
- **Mobile UX:** Standout — the employee app (pay + benefits + insurance) is the hero.

## Things we love
- Insurance/benefits fused into HR/payroll — a genuinely unique bundle.
- Polished, mobile-first employee experience.
- Clean modern SaaS feel and onboarding.

## Things we hate
- UAE-centric; KSA compliance depth (GOSI/Mudad/Qiwa/EOS) is a gap.
- Payroll not transparently reproducible.
- HR-core depth can trail the benefits emphasis.

## Workflows
- **Payroll (+ WPS/GOSI):** Salary setup → run → WPS/SIF (UAE strong) → bank routing; KSA GOSI shallower. Not line-item-traceable.
- **Attendance/Leave:** Mobile geofence → leave → payroll feed.
- **Benefits/Insurance:** Enroll → manage policies → submit/track claims in-app (unique).
- **Approvals:** Clean multi-level + notifications.
- **Reports:** Standard set; custom mid-tier.

## Ideas worth stealing
- Bundling adjacent value (benefits/insurance) into the HR/payroll surface to raise stickiness.
- Mobile-first employee app as the primary product touchpoint.
- Surfacing "what's in it for the employee" (pay + benefits) front-and-center.

## Improvements we can make
- **Simpler:** Match their consumer-grade employee app while going deeper on KSA.
- **Faster:** Comparable modern speed.
- **More configurable:** [[Master Data Engine]] no-code vs bounded config.
- **More automated:** Visual [[Workflow Engine]] beyond basic approvals.
- **More scalable:** [[Financial Calculation Engine]] + [[Immutable Ledger]] transparent payroll; deep [[Saudi Compliance Notes|KSA compliance]] Bayzat lacks.
- **More beautiful:** [[Design System]] parity + first-class [[Arabic RTL]] (a likely Bayzat weakness).
