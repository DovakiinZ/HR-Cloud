# ADP Workforce Now

> The payroll heritage giant — bulletproof payroll + compliance and a huge service network, wrapped in a dated, module-stitched UX.
> Up: [[Competitor Index]] · System: [[COMPETITORS]]

## Overview
- **Company:** ADP, Inc. (Roseland, NJ) — decades-deep payroll service bureau.
- **Product:** ADP Workforce Now (mid-market/enterprise HCM: payroll, HR, benefits, time, talent) plus ADP Vantage HCM (large enterprise), RUN (small biz), and ADP GlobalView / Celergo for multinational payroll.
- **Target Market:** US mid-market to enterprise; ADP GlobalView for global payroll. Heritage = payroll + tax + compliance outsourcing.
- **Pricing:** PEPM subscription + payroll processing/tax-filing fees; managed-service options; opaque quote-based pricing 🔄 verify.
- **Strengths:** Payroll accuracy, tax filing & compliance depth, massive service/support network, benefits administration, global payroll reach via GlobalView, brand trust.
- **Weaknesses:** Dated/inconsistent UX, module-stitched feel, upsell-heavy add-ons, reporting friction, talent/HR modules weaker than pure HCM suites, support experience varies.
- **Positioning:** "Payroll and compliance you can trust, at any size." Bought for payroll reliability and managed service, not modern UX.

## UX Notes
- **Navigation:** Module-stitched; inconsistent look/feel across payroll/HR/time/talent; visibly dated.
- **Search:** Basic; not a strength.
- **Dashboard:** Functional home; not modern/customizable at the Workday level.
- **Configuration:** Often requires ADP implementation/service; limited self-serve depth.
- **Automation:** Workflows exist but modest.
- **Performance:** Reliable for payroll; UI can feel sluggish/legacy.
- **Accessibility:** Enterprise commitments; varies by module.
- **Dark Mode:** Not a feature 🔄 verify.
- **Arabic Support:** Limited; global payroll via GlobalView but Arabic/RTL HR UX not a focus. Contrast [[Arabic RTL]].
- **Mobile UX:** ADP Mobile is the strongest surface — pay, tax docs, clock, time-off.

## Things we love
- Payroll + tax + compliance reliability and scale.
- ADP Mobile app for pay/tax/time self-service.
- Massive service network + ADP Marketplace integrations.

## Things we hate
- Dated, inconsistent, module-stitched UX.
- Upsell/add-on-heavy packaging; opaque pricing.
- Reporting friction and dependence on ADP services to configure.

## Customer complaints
Recurring themes (G2/Capterra/TrustRadius/Reddit): clunky/dated interface and module inconsistency; custom reporting is hard; support experience is hit-or-miss and tiered; nickel-and-diming via add-ons; implementation/config leans on ADP services. (Themes only.)

## Feature requests
Modernize and unify the UX; easier self-serve reporting and configuration; transparent pricing without add-on creep; stronger native talent/HR modules; better admin usability.
