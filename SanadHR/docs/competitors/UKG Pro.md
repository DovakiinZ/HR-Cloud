# UKG Pro

> The workforce-management powerhouse — born from Ultimate Software + Kronos, unmatched in time/scheduling/labor, HCM strong but US-centric.
> Up: [[Competitor Index]] · System: [[COMPETITORS]]

## Overview
- **Company:** UKG (Ultimate Kronos Group), formed by the 2020 merger of Ultimate Software (UltiPro) and Kronos.
- **Product:** UKG Pro (HCM: HR, payroll, benefits, talent) + UKG Pro Workforce Management (formerly Dimensions — time, attendance, scheduling, labor) + UKG Ready for mid-market.
- **Target Market:** Large and mid-market North American enterprises, especially shift/hourly-heavy industries (retail, healthcare, manufacturing, hospitality).
- **Pricing:** PEPM subscription, module-based; WFM often the anchor purchase 🔄 verify bands.
- **Strengths:** Best-in-class time & attendance / scheduling / labor management (Kronos heritage), strong US payroll, culture/sentiment tools (UKG legacy in engagement), solid HR core.
- **Weaknesses:** Primarily US/North America focused (limited global + Saudi/GCC payroll), two-platform HCM+WFM stitching, dated screens in places, integration friction between Pro and WFM.
- **Positioning:** "Great place to work" HCM anchored by elite workforce management. Chosen when time/labor complexity is the pain.

## UX Notes
- **Navigation:** Two products (Pro + WFM) with distinct UIs; consistency gap when stitched.
- **Search:** Adequate; not a headline.
- **Dashboard:** Role dashboards; WFM labor/coverage views are strong.
- **Configuration:** WFM pay-rule/accrual configuration is powerful but complex.
- **Automation:** Business processes + scheduling automation.
- **Performance:** Generally solid; large WFM tenants can be heavy.
- **Accessibility:** Enterprise a11y commitments.
- **Dark Mode:** Not a highlight 🔄 verify.
- **Arabic Support:** Limited global focus; Arabic/RTL and Saudi payroll not a core strength. Contrast [[Arabic RTL]].
- **Mobile UX:** Strong for hourly/shift workflows (punch, swap, availability).

## Things we love
- Elite time/attendance/scheduling/labor rules — the WFM gold standard.
- Shift-swap, availability, and coverage UX for hourly workforces.
- Engagement/sentiment DNA (people-first culture positioning).

## Things we hate
- HCM+WFM as two stitched platforms with integration friction.
- US/North-America centricity; weak global + Saudi payroll.
- Complex WFM pay-rule configuration needing specialists.

## Customer complaints
Recurring themes (G2/TrustRadius/Reddit): Pro↔WFM integration and data-sync friction; support quality inconsistency post-merger; complex configuration for pay rules/accruals; reporting can require expertise; limited outside North America. (Themes only.)

## Feature requests
Unify Pro + WFM into one seamless experience; expand global/regional payroll; simpler pay-rule configuration; better cross-module reporting; stronger recruiting.

## Release Notes
Continued Pro + WFM convergence, UKG Bryte AI assistant, scheduling/labor-forecasting investment, engagement tooling 🔄 verify latest release specifics.

## Workflows
- Payroll: WFM hours/exceptions → Pro payroll → pay run (US-centric). Cf. [[Payroll Engine]].
- Attendance/Leave: punches + pay rules + accruals → payroll (its strongest flow). Cf. [[Attendance Payroll Impact]].
- Recruitment: recruiting → hire into Pro.
- Approvals: timecard/leave/schedule approvals. Cf. [[Request Center]].
- Reports: WFM labor analytics + Pro BI.

## Ideas worth stealing
- Depth of attendance→pay-impact rules — directly relevant to [[Attendance Payroll Impact]].
- Shift/scheduling UX for hourly and deskless workers.
- Engagement/sentiment as a first-class signal.

## Improvements we can make
- **Simpler:** One platform (HR + attendance + payroll), not stitched Pro+WFM.
- **Faster:** Native attendance→deduction/overtime already flows into payroll — [[Attendance Payroll Impact]].
- **More configurable:** No-code pay/attendance rules — [[Master Data Engine]] / [[Rule Engine]].
- **More automated:** [[Workflow Engine]] + [[Completion Effects Engine]] across the whole HR lifecycle.
- **More scalable:** Native Saudi/GCC payroll on a reproducible [[Immutable Ledger]] (global-first, not US-first).
- **More beautiful:** Unified RTL [[Design System]] vs two dated UIs — [[Arabic RTL]].
