# Cerkl HR
> Niche HR play centered on employee engagement & internal communications — not a payroll/HRIS suite.
> Up: [[Competitor Index]] · System: [[COMPETITORS]]

> ⚠️ **Treat carefully — smaller/niche, and easily confused.** The best-known "Cerkl" is **Cerkl Broadcast**, a US-based internal-communications / employee-engagement platform (AI-personalized comms, newsletters, multi-channel employee messaging), **not** a payroll/HRIS in the SanadHR sense. Any "Cerkl HR" positioned as a full HR/payroll suite is unconfirmed. **Nearly everything here is 🔄 verify.** Do not present as a direct payroll competitor without confirmation.

## Overview
- **Company:** Cerkl (Cerkl Broadcast) — US internal-comms/engagement vendor. A regional/rebranded "Cerkl HR" HRIS is **unconfirmed**. 🔄 verify which entity is meant and whether it operates in MENA/KSA.
- **Product:** Employee communications & engagement — AI-personalized content delivery, email/SMS/mobile/Teams/Slack/intranet distribution, engagement analytics/measurement. **Not** core HR/payroll. 🔄 verify any HR-suite features.
- **Target Market:** Internal comms / HR-comms teams at mid-to-large orgs (US-centric). 🔄 verify MENA presence.
- **Pricing:** SaaS, comms-platform tiers; not the HRIS pricing shape. 🔄 verify.
- **Strengths:** AI-personalized employee comms, multi-channel reach, engagement measurement — a genuinely differentiated *comms* niche.
- **Weaknesses (as an HR-suite competitor):** No payroll, no Saudi statutory, no attendance/EOS — it competes with the **engagement/comms slice** of an HR suite, not the platform. 🔄 verify.
- **Positioning:** "Reach and engage every employee with personalized internal communications" — an engagement layer, adjacent to HRIS, not a replacement.
