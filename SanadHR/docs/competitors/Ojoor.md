## Overview
- **Company:** Ojoor (اسم يعني "الأجور"/wages) — Saudi-oriented HR/payroll vendor targeting the local SMB segment. 🔄 verify founding, ownership, HQ, funding, headcount.
- **Product:** Cloud HR + payroll for Saudi small/mid businesses — employee records, attendance, leave, payroll with GOSI/WPS handling, ESS. 🔄 verify exact module list and whether attendance hardware is bundled.
- **Target Market:** KSA SMBs; Arabic-first buyers who want local compliance without enterprise cost/complexity. 🔄 verify GCC coverage beyond KSA.
- **Pricing:** Assumed SMB-friendly per-employee/tiered SaaS. 🔄 verify — do not quote figures.
- **Strengths:** Saudi-local focus, Arabic UI, GOSI/WPS orientation, low barrier to entry for small firms. 🔄 verify.
- **Weaknesses:** Limited enterprise depth; likely thin on advanced workflow/analytics/AI; smaller ecosystem, integrations, and support footprint than Jisr/ZenHR; payroll transparency/audit likely basic. 🔄 verify all.
- **Positioning:** Affordable, Arabic-first, compliance-focused HR/payroll for Saudi small businesses. 🔄 verify messaging.
