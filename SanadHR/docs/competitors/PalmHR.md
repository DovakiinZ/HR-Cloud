# PalmHR

> The modern, sleek Saudi HR challenger — design-forward UX, growing fast in the KSA SMB space.
> Up: [[Competitor Index]] · System: [[COMPETITORS]]

## Overview
- **Company:** PalmHR — modern Saudi/GCC HR SaaS positioned on design and employee experience; a newer-generation challenger to the incumbents. 🔄 verify HQ/funding/scale specifics.
- **Product:** Cloud HR platform: core HR, payroll, attendance/leave, self-service, engagement/EX touches — with Saudi localization (GOSI, WPS, End-of-Service).
- **Target Market:** Saudi/GCC SMB and modern mid-market; companies that prioritize a polished, employee-friendly experience.
- **Pricing:** Per-employee-per-month SaaS; SMB-oriented. 🔄 verify exact tiers/PEPM.
- **Strengths:** Sleek, modern UX and mobile-first employee experience; clean onboarding; good Saudi localization for its stage; strong on look-and-feel and EX vs older rivals.
- **Weaknesses:** Younger product — less battle-tested at payroll scale/edge cases than [[Jisr]]/[[Menaitech]]; compliance depth solid but not as exhaustive as pure-KSA compliance leaders; configurability and reporting still maturing; smaller integration/gov-flow surface; payroll not transparently reproducible.
- **Positioning:** "Modern, beautiful HR built for the new Saudi workplace." UX/EX as the moat.

## UX Notes
- **Navigation:** Modern, clean, design-forward — a clear generational step up from legacy rivals.
- **Search:** Adequate.
- **Dashboard:** Attractive, employee-centric.
- **Configuration:** Friendly; guided; bounded.
- **Automation:** Basic rule-based approvals/reminders.
- **Performance:** Snappy, modern feel.
- **Accessibility:** Standard.
- **Dark Mode:** 🔄 verify (modern product — plausible).
- **Arabic Support:** Good Arabic/RTL with modern polish; still, a refined SanadHR RTL should target better bilingual parity. See [[Arabic RTL]].
- **Mobile UX:** Standout — mobile-first EX is the headline.

## Things we love
- The most design-forward UX/EX among the KSA-focused challengers.
- Excellent mobile + self-service employee experience.
- Feels like a modern SaaS product, not a legacy HRIS.

## Things we hate
- Younger payroll engine — less proven at scale/edge cases.
- Compliance/integration surface smaller than incumbents.
- Reporting/configurability still maturing; payroll not reproducible.

## Workflows
- **Payroll (+ WPS/GOSI):** Salary setup → run → GOSI + WPS outputs → bank routing. Modern UI; not line-item-traceable.
- **Attendance/Leave:** Mobile geofence check-in → leave → payroll feed.
- **Recruitment:** Light requisition → hire → onboard.
- **Approvals:** Clean multi-level + notifications.
- **Reports:** Standard set; custom maturing.

## Ideas worth stealing
- Employee-experience-first framing (make ESS/mobile the hero).
- Modern onboarding journeys and polished micro-interactions.
- Design-forward dashboards that feel consumer-grade.

## Improvements we can make
- **Simpler:** Match EX polish while adding depth.
- **Faster:** Comparable modern speed, plus deeper engine.
- **More configurable:** [[Master Data Engine]] no-code vs bounded config.
- **More automated:** Visual [[Workflow Engine]] beyond basic approvals.
- **More scalable:** [[Financial Calculation Engine]] + [[Immutable Ledger]] give proven, transparent payroll a young engine lacks.
- **More beautiful:** [[Design System]] parity/superiority + first-class [[Arabic RTL]] bilingual polish.
